Every year, tons of perfectly edible tomatoes are discarded — not because they're bad, but because they don't meet cosmetic standards. F.A.R.M. was founded on a simple belief: no tomato should go to waste.

Kamatamis Spread is the answer. We rescue surplus, oddly-shaped, and blemished tomatoes from Bukidnon farms and transform them into a premium, nutrient-rich spread. What was once considered waste becomes a product that nourishes communities.

The Root of the Problem

In the Philippines, particularly in tomato-growing regions like Bukidnon, post-harvest losses are staggering. Farmers often lose between 20% to 40% of their harvest due to:

  • ✓ Cosmetic rejection (odd shapes, minor blemishes)
  • ✓ Lack of cold chain infrastructure during transit
  • ✓ Oversupply flooding local markets
  • ✓ Inadequate processing facilities
  • ✓ Price volatility and trader exploitation
  • ✓ Limited market access for small farmers

3 Long-Term Benefits

Why choosing Kamatamis creates lasting change

Alternative Solutions for Surplus Produce

By creating a stable market for "imperfect" tomatoes, Kamatamis provides farmers with an alternative outlet that doesn't rely on volatile fresh market prices.

Stable Livelihood for Local Farmers

F.A.R.M. contracts directly with Bukidnon farmers, guaranteeing purchase of their surplus tomatoes at fair prices.

Consumer Access to Health Benefits

Kamatamis Spread is rich in lycopene, Vitamin C, and potassium — nutrients preserved through slow-cooking for heart health, immunity, and skin wellness.
