Oatmeal & Yogurt Boost

Stir a spoonful into your morning bowl for natural sweetness, a hint of spice, and a boost of lycopene and Vitamin C — no refined sugar needed.

Healthy 5 min prep
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1–2 tbsp Kamatamis Spread
  • 1 cup cooked oatmeal or plain yogurt
  • Optional: nuts, seeds, or fresh fruit

Good For

A filling, nutrient-dense breakfast or post-workout snack.

How to Make It

1

Prepare your oatmeal or scoop plain yogurt into a bowl.

2

Add 1–2 tablespoons of Kamatamis Spread and stir until evenly blended.

3

Top with nuts, seeds, or fresh fruit for extra texture.

4

Enjoy warm or chilled — both work beautifully.
