BBQ Glaze Alternative

Swap your usual BBQ sauce for Kamatamis and give it a Filipino twist. Mix with calamansi juice and chili paste for a dipping sauce that shines with lechon or chicken inasal.

Pinoy Flavor Grilling
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 tbsp Kamatamis Spread
  • Juice of 3 calamansi
  • 1 tsp chili paste (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Pinch of brown sugar

Good For

Lechon, chicken inasal, grilled pork belly, and skewers.

How to Make It

1

Combine Kamatamis, calamansi juice, chili paste, soy sauce, and sugar in a bowl.

2

Whisk until the sauce is smooth and glossy.

3

Taste and adjust the heat or sweetness to your liking.

4

Serve as a dipping sauce or brush on during grilling.
