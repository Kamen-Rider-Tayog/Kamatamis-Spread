Classic Bread Spread

The simplest way to enjoy Kamatamis — spread it on warm bread and let the gentle heat release the aroma of ginger and cinnamon.

Quick & Easy Breakfast
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1–2 tbsp Kamatamis Spread (Classic or Spicy-Chili)
  • Pandesal, sourdough, toast, or croissants
  • Optional: butter or cream cheese

Good For

Breakfast, merienda, and quick snacks for the whole family.

How to Make It

1

Toast or lightly warm your bread until the surface is crisp but the inside stays soft.

2

Spoon 1–2 tablespoons of Kamatamis Spread evenly across the surface.

3

For extra richness, add a thin layer of butter or cream cheese first, then the spread.

4

Serve immediately while the bread is still warm.
