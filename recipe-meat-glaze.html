Sweet & Spicy Meat Glaze

Combine Kamatamis with soy sauce, garlic, and vinegar, then brush it on during the final minutes of grilling for a glossy, caramelized finish.

Trending Spicy
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp Kamatamis Spread (Spicy-Chili works best)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Pork, chicken, or salmon

Good For

Weekend barbecues, family gatherings, and holiday feasts.

How to Make It

1

Whisk Kamatamis, soy sauce, garlic, and vinegar into a smooth glaze.

2

Season and grill your meat until almost cooked through.

3

Brush the glaze on during the last 5 minutes, turning to coat both sides.

4

Rest briefly, then serve with extra glaze on the side.
