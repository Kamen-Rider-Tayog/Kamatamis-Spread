Why Kamatamis Matters

Three pillars of our mission

Circular Economy

Upcycled Surplus Tomatoes

We rescue oddly-shaped, blemished, and surplus tomatoes that would otherwise go to waste — up to 37.71% of harvest lost in traditional supply chains.

Community Impact

Farm-to-Jar Livelihood Support

Stable demand for surplus produce means fair farmgate prices for Bukidnon farmers, protecting them from prices dropping to P4–P6/kg during oversupply.

Nutrition

Extended Shelf Life & High Nutrition

Slow-cooked concentration preserves lycopene, Vitamin C, and potassium — delivering more nutrients per spoonful than fresh tomatoes.
