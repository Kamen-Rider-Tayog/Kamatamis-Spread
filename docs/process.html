From Farm to Jar

The Kamatamis production process

Boil & Peel

Fresh surplus tomatoes from Bukidnon farms are thoroughly washed, boiled, and peeled to remove skins.

Grind to Paste

Peeled tomatoes are ground into a fine, uniform paste. The consistency is carefully monitored to achieve the perfect texture — smooth yet thick enough to spread.

Strain & Reduce to 1/3

The tomato paste is strained and slowly simmered until reduced to one-third of its original volume. This concentration intensifies the natural sweetness and boosts lycopene content.

Add Spices & Lemon Juice

Brown sugar, cinnamon, fresh ginger, and lemon juice are carefully incorporated. For the Spicy-Chili variant, chili powder and flakes are added at this stage for a balanced kick.

Cool & Glass Jar Packaging

The finished spread cools naturally before being packaged into 150ml eco-friendly glass jars. Each jar is sealed with a metal lid, labeled, and quality-checked.

Recipe Suggestions

Creative ways to enjoy Kamatamis

Classic Bread Spread

Spread Kamatamis generously on warm pandesal, toast, or croissants. The heat from the bread gently melts the jam, releasing the aromatic notes of ginger and cinnamon.

Quick & Easy Breakfast

Oatmeal & Yogurt Boost

Stir 1–2 tablespoons into your morning oatmeal or yogurt. Adds natural sweetness, a hint of spice, and a nutritional boost of lycopene and Vitamin C without refined sugars.

Healthy 5 min prep

Sweet & Spicy Meat Glaze

Combine Kamatamis with soy sauce, garlic, and a dash of vinegar. Brush on pork, chicken, or salmon during the last 5 minutes of grilling for a caramelized, tangy glaze.

Trending Spicy

BBQ Glaze Alternative

Replace your usual BBQ sauce with Kamatamis for a Filipino twist. Mix with calamansi juice and chili paste for a dipping sauce that pairs with lechon or chicken inasal.

Pinoy Flavor Grilling

Cheese Board Condiment

Pair Kamatamis with creamy brie, sharp cheddar, or kesong puti. The sweet-tart balance cuts through rich cheese flavors, elevating any charcuterie board.

Entertaining Hosting

Stir-Fry & Noodle Add-in

Add a tablespoon of Kamatamis to stir-fried noodles or fried rice during the last minute of cooking. It caramelizes beautifully, adding depth and a subtle spicy kick.
