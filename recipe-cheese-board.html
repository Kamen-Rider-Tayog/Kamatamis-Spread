Cheese Board Condiment

The sweet-tart balance of Kamatamis cuts through rich cheese flavors, elevating any charcuterie board with a bright, savory-sweet accent.

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 small bowl of Kamatamis Spread
  • Brie, sharp cheddar, or kesong puti
  • Crackers or toasted baguette slices
  • Optional: grapes, nuts, and honey

Good For

Parties, wine nights, and relaxed weekend gatherings.

How to Make It

1

Arrange your cheeses and crackers on a board or platter.

2

Place a small bowl of Kamatamis Spread at the center of the board.

3

Fill the gaps with grapes, nuts, and a drizzle of honey.

4

Let guests spoon the spread over cheese and crackers as they like.
