Stir-Fry & Noodle Add-in

Add a tablespoon of Kamatamis to stir-fried noodles or fried rice during the last minute of cooking. It caramelizes beautifully, adding depth and a subtle spicy kick.

Spicy Quick Meal
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Kamatamis Spread (Spicy-Chili recommended)
  • Cooked noodles or day-old rice
  • Mixed vegetables and protein of choice
  • Soy sauce and garlic to taste

Good For

Fast weeknight dinners and using up leftover rice or noodles.

How to Make It

1

Heat your pan and sauté garlic, vegetables, and protein as usual.

2

Add noodles or rice and toss to combine.

3

Stir in a tablespoon of Kamatamis during the last minute of cooking.

4

Toss until evenly coated and serve hot.
